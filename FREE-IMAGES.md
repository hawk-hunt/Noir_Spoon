# Free Food Images Download Guide

This guide helps you quickly find and download free, high-quality food images for your restaurant menu.

## ✅ Best Free Stock Photo Sites (No Attribution Required)

### 1. **Unsplash** (unsplash.com)
- **Best for**: Professional, high-quality food photography
- **License**: Free for commercial use
- **Search**: "Mexican tacos", "breakfast pancakes", "lasagna"
- **Quality**: 4000x3000px and larger

### 2. **Pexels** (pexels.com)
- **Best for**: Diverse food categories
- **License**: Free for commercial and personal use
- **Collections**: "Food", "Mexican cuisine", "Breakfast"
- **Quality**: High-resolution, all free for download

### 3. **Pixabay** (pixabay.com)
- **Best for**: Food and restaurant images
- **License**: Free for commercial use
- **Categories**: Food & Drink, Cooking, Breakfast
- **Quality**: Professional stock photos

### 4. **Food.images** (food.images.com)
- **Best for**: Specialized food photography
- **License**: Creative Commons
- **Quality**: Professional culinary photos

## 📥 How to Download and Add Images

### Step 1: Download Image
1. Visit one of the sites above
2. Search for your food item
3. Preview the image
4. Click "Download" button
5. Save to your computer

### Step 2: Prepare Image
```bash
# Recommended image optimization:
- Size: 800x600 px or 1000x750 px
- Format: JPG (for web)
- File size: <500KB
- Quality: 85% compression
```

### Step 3: Upload to Your Menu
1. Go to `admin/menu-manager.php`
2. Fill in the menu item details
3. Select the downloaded image
4. Click "Add Item"

## 📋 Quick Checklists

### Before Adding Images:
- [ ] Image is food-related and appealing
- [ ] Image is royalty-free/free for commercial use
- [ ] Image resolution is at least 600px wide
- [ ] File size is less than 5MB
- [ ] Image is JPG or PNG format
- [ ] License allows restaurant use

### Image Quality Checklist:
- [ ] Photo is sharp and in-focus
- [ ] Colors look natural and appetizing
- [ ] Lighting is professional (no shadows or glare)
- [ ] Food is the main subject
- [ ] Composition is balanced
- [ ] No watermarks or branding

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Download in Bulk**: Most sites let you download multiple images
2. **Create Collections**: Save food images to a folder for organization
3. **Rename Files**: Use descriptive names (e.g., tacos-al-pastor.jpg)
4. **Backup**: Keep originals before compression
5. **Test on Mobile**: Ensure images look good on phones
6. **Update Regularly**: Refresh images seasonally
